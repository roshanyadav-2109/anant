# Third-party assets

## Icons — Solar Icon Set

Primary UI icons are from the **Solar Icon Set** (Bold style), by 480 Design.

- License: **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Source: https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736 · via https://icon-sets.iconify.design/solar/
- The glyph data is embedded in `src/icons/solar-data.ts`.

The Anant brand mark and the neutral connector stand-in glyphs in
`src/icons/index.tsx` are original to this project.

## Connector brand logos

Vendor marks under `public/connectors/` (Slack, Gmail, Outlook, Google Drive,
Notion, Google Calendar, Linear, GitHub, Microsoft Teams) are the trademarks of
their respective owners, used to identify each integration. For production,
re-source each from the vendor's official brand/press kit and follow its usage
guidelines.
